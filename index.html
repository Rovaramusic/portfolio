Roberto van Raamsdonk

Hailing from Amsterdam, Rovara (Roberto van Raamsdonk) is a fast-rising force in the global Tech House scene. Known for his infectious, club-focused tracks and rolling peak-time energy, he delivers hypnotic grooves that move dancefloors worldwide.

When he’s not in the studio or behind the decks, you can catch his weekly radio show every Friday 7–8 PM on HouseBeats FM.
